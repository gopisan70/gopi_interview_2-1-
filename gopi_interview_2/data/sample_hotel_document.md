## 1. About the Hotel

The Grand Azure Bay Hotel is a five-star seaside resort with 120 rooms and suites overlooking Azure Bay. The hotel opened in 2012 and was fully renovated in 2023. Facilities include an infinity pool, a spa and wellness centre, a fitness studio open 24 hours, two restaurants, a rooftop bar, and 600 square metres of meeting and event space. Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the property.

## 2. Location and Getting There

The hotel is located at 18 Marina Promenade, Azure Bay, directly on the waterfront and a five-minute walk from the Old Town. Azure Bay International Airport is 22 kilometres away, roughly 30 minutes by car. The hotel offers a private airport transfer for 45 dollars each way; bookings should be made at least 24 hours in advance through the concierge. Azure Bay Central railway station is 3 kilometres from the hotel, and the number 12 bus stops directly outside the main entrance. On-site underground parking is available for 20 dollars per night, and electric vehicle charging points are provided free of charge.

## 3. Rooms and Rates

The hotel offers three room categories. Standard Rooms are 28 square metres with a queen bed and a garden or city view, from 120 dollars per night. Deluxe Rooms are 38 square metres with a king bed, a private balcony and a sea view, from 190 dollars per night. Suites are 65 square metres with a separate living room, a marble bathroom with a bathtub, and a panoramic bay view, from 320 dollars per night. All rooms include air conditioning, a minibar, a safe, a Nespresso machine and blackout curtains. Interconnecting rooms and accessible rooms are available on request.

## 4. Hygiene and Safety

The Grand Azure Bay Hotel follows an enhanced cleaning protocol. All guest rooms are cleaned daily with hospital-grade disinfectants, and high-touch surfaces such as door handles, remote controls, light switches and lift buttons are sanitised every two hours in public areas. Rooms are left vacant for a minimum of four hours between guests and are treated with electrostatic sprayers before a new check-in. Bed linen and towels are laundered at 70 degrees Celsius. Hand-sanitiser stations are placed at every entrance, at the reception desk, in the lifts and in all restaurants. Kitchen staff hold current food-safety certifications and the kitchens are independently audited every quarter. The hotel is fully non-smoking, is equipped with smoke detectors and sprinklers in every room, and has a first-aid trained staff member on duty 24 hours a day. A doctor can be called to the hotel at any time. The pool water is tested three times a day and a lifeguard is on duty from 8:00 to 20:00.

## 5. Dining

The Azure Restaurant serves breakfast from 6:30 to 10:30 and dinner from 18:30 to 22:30. The signature dish of the Grand Azure Bay Hotel is the Azure Bay Grilled Sea Bass, a locally caught sea bass served with saffron risotto, charred lemon and a herb butter sauce, prepared by executive chef Marco Bellini. The restaurant offers a dedicated vegetarian menu with at least six main courses, including a wild-mushroom risotto and a roasted-aubergine tagine; vegan and gluten-free options are available on request, and every menu item is clearly labelled with allergen information. The Bayside Grill by the pool serves light lunches, salads and wood-fired pizzas from 12:00 to 17:00. The Skyline Rooftop Bar is open from 17:00 until midnight and serves cocktails and tapas. Room service is available 24 hours a day. Breakfast is included in all room rates and features a buffet with fresh fruit, pastries, a live egg station and local specialities.

## 6. Reservation Policies

Check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00. Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur a fee of 40 dollars. A valid photo ID and a credit card are required at check-in. Reservations can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in date. Cancellations made less than 48 hours before check-in are charged one night's stay, and no-shows are charged the full amount of the reservation. Non-refundable promotional rates cannot be cancelled or modified. Modifications to the dates of a booking are free of charge when made more than 48 hours before arrival and are subject to availability. The maximum length of stay for a single booking is 30 nights. Children under 12 stay free when sharing a room with their parents. Pets are not permitted, with the exception of certified assistance animals.

## 7. Payment

The hotel accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express and cash in the local currency. A pre-authorisation of 100 dollars per night is taken at check-in to cover incidental charges. All rates include taxes and service charges unless otherwise stated.

## 8. Contact

For assistance, guests can contact the front desk by dialling 0 from any room telephone, or reach the reservations team at +1 555 0100 between 8:00 and 20:00 daily.
